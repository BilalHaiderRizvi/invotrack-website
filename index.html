FAQ

Common questions

How do I subscribe?

Choose a plan above and message us on WhatsApp or email. Once payment is received we activate the plan on the account you signed up with. There's nothing to install beyond the app.

What happens if my plan expires?

You keep access to everything you've already created. You can still open, view and share all your past invoices. Creating new invoices needs an active plan.

Where are my invoices stored?

Your account, business profile and expenses are stored securely in the cloud with your account. Invoices and their PDFs are currently saved on your phone, so share or save copies of important PDFs. Cloud sync for invoices is on our roadmap.

Does it handle CGST, SGST and IGST?

Yes. Each item can carry its own HSN/SAC code and GST rates. The invoice shows the taxable value, the CGST, SGST or IGST amounts, and a GST summary table.

Can I change my business details later?

Yes. Your business name, address, contact and bank details can be updated any time from Settings → Business Profile. Your GSTIN is fixed once saved, because it identifies your business. Contact us if it genuinely needs correcting.

Can I use my own invoice numbers?

Yes. InvoTrack suggests the next number automatically, and you can edit it to match your existing series before generating the invoice.

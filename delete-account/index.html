Delete your InvoTrack account

How to request deletion of your InvoTrack account and data, published by .

How to request deletion

  1. From the email address you use to sign in to InvoTrack, email with the subject "Delete my InvoTrack account".
  2. We'll reply to confirm the request came from you.
  3. Once confirmed, we delete your account and data within 30 days and email you when it's done.

Before you delete: invoices are stored on your phone, not on our servers. Share or save copies of any invoice PDFs you need for your records first. You'll need them for GST compliance.

What is deleted

  • Your InvoTrack login (email address and password)
  • Your business profile, including name, contact details, address, GSTIN and bank / UPI details
  • All expenses you recorded
  • Your subscription record

What is kept, and for how long

  • Payment records for plans you bought, such as the amount, date and the invoice we issued you, are kept for as long as Indian GST and income-tax law requires. They are then deleted.
  • Invoices and PDFs on your phone are not on our servers, so we can't delete them for you. To remove them, uninstall the App or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → InvoTrack → Storage. PDFs saved to your Downloads folder must be deleted separately.

Deleting some data without closing your account

You can delete individual invoices and expenses yourself inside the App. To have your cloud data erased while keeping your account, email us with the subject "Delete my InvoTrack data" and tell us what you'd like removed.
